Notes

Fixed-length AIs (e.g. 01, 17) need no separator.

Variable-length AIs (e.g. 10, 21) get a real GS (0x1D) byte automatically inserted when another field follows — never typed as text.

The symbol always starts with a true FNC1 in the first position, marking it as GS1 data to the scanner.
AI definitions and field rules derived from the GS1 Barcode Syntax Dictionary (gs1/gs1-syntax-dictionary, GS1 AISBL). For the authoritative, complete specification refer to the GS1 General Specifications and ref.gs1.org/ai.
